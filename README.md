## 01 / About

Cheers everyone! I'm Emiliano, a **Computer Science student at California State University, Fullerton** and **Vice Chair of the ACM SIGGRAPH Student Chapter at CSUF**.

My background spans computer science, psychology, and digital art. I'm interested in how people think, create, and interact with technology—and how software engineering can support that process. My current interests include **computer graphics, software architecture, human-computer interaction, and ethical AI integration**.

I'm currently leading architecture and feature design for **Blendity**, a 3D creative application. Across my work, I aim to connect creative practice with thoughtful engineering, clear documentation, and usable tools.

### 02.1 / Blendity

**3D creative software · October 2026–present**  
`C++` `Vulkan` `GLSL` `Python` `Blender`

A 3D creative application combining Unity-style interaction with Blender-inspired modeling and rendering workflows.

- Lead software architecture and feature design, and review C++ changes, tests, and documentation before integration.
- Oversee development of workflows for modeling, sculpting, retopology, rigging, animation, and simulation.

Development uses AI assistance under my direction and review. The project's disclosure explains how responsibilities are divided.
